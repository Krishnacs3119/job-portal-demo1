## 3. Untested Scenarios & Justifications

In accordance with the safety instructions for this QA engagement, the following items were intentionally excluded from automated browser testing:

| Untested Item | Justification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **External Email Inbox Delivery (Brevo / SMTP / Gmail)** | Testing required verifying real inbound messages in third-party personal or external webmail services (Gmail, Brevo), which is strictly prohibited by Safety Rule 1 (*"NEVER open supabase.com, brevo.com, gmail, google.com accounts, or any other site"*). The internal UI dispatch state, selection claim lock (`claim_selection_email`), and DB sent timestamps (`selection_email_sent_at`) were verified directly within the platform. |
| **Third-Party Google OAuth Login ("Continue with Google")** | Safety Rule 1 explicitly mandates: *"If a page asks to log in to Google, STOP and skip that test. Do NOT use 'Continue with Google' at all."* All authentication was validated via native email and password flows. |
| **SMS Phone OTP Verification Delivery** | Phone OTP involves external telecom carrier gateways and real phone SMS message receipt. Real phone SMS numbers and carrier APIs were excluded to prevent SMS costs and adhere to local testing boundaries. |
| **Cloudflare Turnstile Live Challenge Bypass** | The dev environment utilizes Turnstile in test/mock mode (`1x00000000000000000000AA`). Testing live interactive anti-bot CAPTCHAs was avoided per safety guidelines. |
